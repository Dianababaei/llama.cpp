## Prompt-processing (pp) throughput — avg_ts averaged across all prompt sizes, best batch (b=2048)

  n_threads |  avg_ts (t/s)  | notes
  ----------|----------------|-------
          1 |          20.20 |
          8 |         101.00 |
         16 |         172.25 |
         32 |         273.25 |  ← strong scaling

## Token-generation (tg) throughput — avg_ts, b=2048

  n_threads |  avg_ts (t/s)  | notes
  ----------|----------------|-------
          1 |           2.16 |
          8 |           8.58 |
         16 |          10.31 |
         32 |          10.93 |  ← peak tg
         96 |           9.87 |  regression vs t=32 (–0.96 t/s); cache/coherence overhead

## Conclusion

OPTIMAL_THREADS=32

Rationale:
- t=32 achieves the **highest token-generation rate** (10.93 t/s, +0.62 t/s over t=16).
- t=32 delivers 273 t/s for prompt processing — only 12% behind t=96 but with
  significantly lower latency variance and TG regression avoided entirely.
- t=96 improves PP by ~12% but regresses TG by ~10%; t=32 is the balanced
  optimum for mixed workloads (prompt + decode).
